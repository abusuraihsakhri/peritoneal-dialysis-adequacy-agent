Method & interpretation

PET calculation and historical bands

4-hour D/P creatinine = dialysate creatinine at 4 hours / plasma creatinine. Illustrative historical categories: Low (below 0.50), low-average (0.50 to below 0.65), high-average (0.65 to below 0.81), high (0.81 and above). These categorical bounds are not universally applicable; follow your center's PET protocol and reference distribution.

Weekly Kt/V calculation

Peritoneal weekly Kt/V = 7 × (pooled 24-hour dialysate urea / serum urea) × 24-hour effluent volume (L) / V (L). Optional renal Kt/V is computed analogously using urine urea and 24-hour urine volume. Total weekly Kt/V is their sum. Input V must be a separately derived estimate; this tool does not calculate body water.

Sources and clinical context

ISPD recommendations: Membrane evaluation (2021) and Goal-directed PD prescription (2020). Numerical clearance by itself does not establish acceptable dialysis. Consider patient-reported outcomes, symptoms, residual kidney function, volume control, biochemical measures and treatment goals.
